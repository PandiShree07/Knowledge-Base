# Data Privacy Guidelines

Collect and process only information needed for a legitimate purpose. Limit access to sensitive data, define retention periods, and use approved channels for sharing. Use synthetic or anonymized data for demonstrations and development. Requests to correct or delete personal information should follow the organization's formal process and applicable law.
