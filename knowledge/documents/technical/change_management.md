# Change Management Standard

Changes should have an owner, a description of expected impact, an appropriate test plan, and a rollback approach. Higher-risk changes require stronger review and explicit approval. Communicate planned downtime and customer impact before release. Verify health after deployment and document incidents or unexpected results.
