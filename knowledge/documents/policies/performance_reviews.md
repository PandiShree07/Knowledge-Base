# Performance Review Guidelines

Performance reviews should connect role expectations, agreed goals, demonstrated outcomes, collaboration, and development. Managers should provide specific examples and feedback throughout the review period rather than relying only on recent events. Employees should be given an opportunity to share achievements, constraints, and learning goals. Ratings should follow the organization's published framework and should not be invented from unrelated database fields.
