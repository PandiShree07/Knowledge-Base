# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Business expenses must be necessary, reasonable, and connected to an approved business purpose. Employees should submit itemized receipts and a short explanation through the expense workflow. Managers verify business purpose and budget ownership; Finance checks policy compliance and required evidence. Personal expenses must not be submitted as business expenses. Exceptions require written approval from the appropriate budget owner.
