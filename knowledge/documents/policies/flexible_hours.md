# Flexible Working Hours

Flexible schedules may be agreed with a manager when customer coverage, collaboration, and delivery commitments remain supported. Teams should document core collaboration hours, meeting expectations, and escalation coverage. Employees should accurately record attendance and working time when required. Flexibility does not remove the need to comply with applicable working-time rules or approved leave procedures.
