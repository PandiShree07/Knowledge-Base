# Code of Conduct

Employees are expected to act honestly, treat colleagues respectfully, protect confidential information, and disclose potential conflicts of interest. Harassment, retaliation, fraud, and deliberate misuse of company resources are not acceptable. Concerns should be raised through a manager, HR, or the designated reporting channel. Reports should be handled discreetly and reviewed fairly.
