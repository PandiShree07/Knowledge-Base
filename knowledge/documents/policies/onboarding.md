# Employee Onboarding Process

Before a new employee starts, the hiring manager coordinates equipment, required account access, team introductions, and an initial work plan. The first week should include security training, policy orientation, role expectations, and a named point of contact. Access should follow least privilege and be granted only after appropriate approvals. Managers should schedule early check-ins to identify blockers.
