# Business Travel Policy

Business travel should have a clear business purpose and receive approval before booking. Employees should use approved booking channels when available, choose reasonable options, and retain itemized receipts. Travelers should review destination safety guidance and know how to contact the company in an emergency. Changes that materially increase cost or risk require manager approval.
