# Annual Leave Policy

Employees should request annual leave through the leave management workflow before making non-refundable travel arrangements. Managers review team coverage, delivery milestones, and fairness across the team. A request is not approved until its status is marked approved. Pending requests must not be counted as approved leave. Employees should coordinate handover notes for active work before their leave begins. Carryover rules depend on the employee's applicable employment terms; this demo knowledge base does not define a universal carryover entitlement. Questions about a person's remaining balance must be answered from the leave records in MySQL, not inferred from this policy.
