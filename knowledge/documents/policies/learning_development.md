# Learning and Development Policy

Employees should discuss development goals with their manager and choose learning activities connected to role needs or agreed career objectives. Training may include courses, mentoring, documentation, workshops, and practical projects. Share useful lessons with teammates and apply company data-handling rules to learning exercises.
