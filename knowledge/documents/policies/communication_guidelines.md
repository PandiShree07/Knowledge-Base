# Internal Communication Guidelines

Internal communication should state the context, the requested action, the owner, and the relevant deadline. Use concise subject lines and separate confirmed facts from assumptions. Respect working hours and choose synchronous meetings only when an asynchronous message is insufficient. Keep confidential details in approved channels.
