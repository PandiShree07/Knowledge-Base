# Employee Offboarding Checklist

Offboarding should be coordinated by the manager, HR, IT, and relevant system owners. The plan includes knowledge transfer, return of company assets, final administrative steps, and timely revocation of access based on the approved departure schedule. Shared credentials must not be transferred informally. Sensitive departure details should be shared only with people who need them.
