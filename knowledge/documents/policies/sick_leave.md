# Sick Leave Policy

Employees who cannot work due to illness should notify their manager as early as practical and record the absence through the approved workflow. Managers should handle medical details confidentially and request only documentation permitted by company policy and local law. Sick leave entries should use the sick leave type and the correct date range. Repeated or extended absences may require a confidential HR discussion. A record marked pending is not a final approval.
