# Meeting Guidelines

Meetings should have a clear purpose, relevant attendees, and an agenda when useful. Share pre-reading early enough for participants to prepare. Start and end on time, record decisions and owners, and distinguish decisions from open questions. Cancel meetings that no longer have a useful outcome.
