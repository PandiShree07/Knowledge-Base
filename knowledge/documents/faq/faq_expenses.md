# FAQ: Expense Claims

A complete expense claim usually includes date, vendor, amount, currency, business purpose, cost center, and an itemized receipt. Follow the approval workflow and submit claims within the stated deadline. If a receipt is unavailable, explain why and follow the exception process rather than fabricating evidence.
