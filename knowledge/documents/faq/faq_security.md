# FAQ: Reporting a Security Concern

Report suspected security problems immediately through the approved incident channel. Include what happened, when it was noticed, the affected system, and any relevant non-sensitive evidence. Do not post secrets or credentials in a public ticket. Do not attempt unauthorized access to verify the issue.
