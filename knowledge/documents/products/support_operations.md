# Support Operations Guide

Support teams should acknowledge requests, classify impact and urgency, gather reproducible details, and communicate the next step. Escalate incidents that affect multiple users, security, or critical business functions. Keep updates factual and avoid promising a resolution time without evidence. Record the final cause and remediation when the issue is resolved.
