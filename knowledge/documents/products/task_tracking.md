# Task Tracking Module

Tasks are associated with projects and assignees. Statuses include todo, in_progress, blocked, and completed. Estimated hours are planning estimates rather than actual time spent. Useful reports include overdue tasks, blocked work, status distribution, and workload by assignee. Avoid interpreting task counts alone as a complete measure of productivity.
