# Employee Directory

The demo employee directory includes a synthetic employee ID, name, example.test email, department, job title, manager name, location, hire date, salary band, and employment status. These records are generated for practice and do not represent real people. Use department and location for aggregate examples. Do not infer personal characteristics from names or other unrelated fields.
